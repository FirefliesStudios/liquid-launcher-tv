# Testing Liquid Launcher TV

Thanks for helping test. It takes about five minutes to set up.

**You need:** an Android TV or Google TV device (Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV
Streamer, or a TV with Android TV built in), and the Google account you gave me signed in
on that TV.

---

## What the app is

Liquid Launcher TV replaces your TV's home screen. Instead of the row of
recommendations your TV came with, you get your own apps, arranged how you want them,
and it is built entirely for the remote — nothing needs a touchscreen or a hidden
gesture.

What you will see:

- **Continue watching** — a full-screen row of what to pick up next, gathered from the
  streaming apps you already use. It can be switched off if you would rather have a
  clean background.
- **A dock you arrange** — your most-used apps along the bottom. Hold **OK** on any card
  to move it, hide it, or open its app info. Scroll down and the dock slides away to
  show every app you have.
- **Themes and a screensaver** — sixteen background gradients, and a screensaver with
  the clock, date and your local weather. Both free.
- **Remote buttons** — options for what the Home and Back buttons do, and whether the
  launcher starts with your TV. All optional, all in Settings.
- **HDMI and AV inputs** show up as ordinary cards, so you can move or hide them like
  any app.

Your TV's own language is used automatically — over 80 are included, and Arabic, Hebrew,
Persian and Urdu lay out right-to-left as they should.

**Free vs paid.** The launcher is free and stays free. One purchase — not a subscription
— unlocks your own photo or a looping video as the wallpaper, icon packs, a custom icon
for any single app, and the ambient photo screensaver.

**Those are free for you, and worth trying.** Everyone in the testers group is set up so
the purchase runs as a test: the flow works, the features unlock, and nothing is charged.
Billing is the part that is hardest for me to test on my own, so exercising it genuinely
helps.

> **Check before you confirm.** The payment method should read *"Test card, always
> approves"*. If it shows your own card instead, stop and tell me — that means the setup
> is wrong at my end, and you should not be charged for testing this.

**Privacy.** No account, no sign-in, no adverts, no tracking, and nothing about you is
sent anywhere. Only two things ever go online: the screensaver weather, which knows only
the city you type in yourself, and the ambient photos. Turn both off and the app never
connects at all.

## 1. Join the test (on your phone or computer)

Join the testers group while signed in to **the same Google account that is on your TV**:

**https://groups.google.com/g/liquid-launcher-tv-testers**

If it says you are already a member, that is fine — carry on.

> The account matters more than anything else here. If the TV is signed in to a different
> Google account than the one that joined the group, the app will never appear.

---

## 2. Opt in to the test

Still in the same browser, signed in to the same account, open:

**https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.jd.liquidlauncher.tv**

Press **Become a tester**.

This step is easy to skip and nothing works without it — until you opt in here, Google
Play will tell you the app does not exist.

---

## 3. Install it onto the TV (easiest way)

Once you have opted in, that same page shows a **Download it on Google Play** link.
Follow it, press **Install**, then pick your **TV** from the device list that appears.

The TV does not need to be on — it will install next time it is switched on and connected
to Wi-Fi.

**If Play says "item not found":** you have most likely not done step 2 yet — that is
the usual cause. Otherwise you are signed in to the wrong Google account, or it has not
finished processing: wait 10-15 minutes and try again. It can occasionally take a couple
of hours the first time.

> **The app may not be called "Liquid Launcher TV" yet.** Until Google finishes reviewing
> it, the store shows a placeholder name — `com.jd.liquidlauncher.tv (unreviewed)`. That is
> the right app. The proper name and icon appear once the review completes.

### Alternative: install directly on the TV

On the TV, open **Google Play Store → Search** and search for *Liquid Launcher* — or for
`com.jd.liquidlauncher.tv` if the placeholder name is still showing.

Test builds do not always appear in TV search, so if it is not there, use the phone or
computer method above instead. That always works.

---

## 4. Open it

After installing, find **Liquid Launcher TV** in your TV's apps row and open it.

You will be asked a few setup questions the first time. You can say **Not now** to any of
them and change your mind later in Settings.

### Making it your home screen (optional)

The app works fine opened manually. If you want it to be your actual home screen, the
setup asks about the **Home button** — follow whichever option it offers on your TV.
Some TVs allow it directly; others need the accessibility toggle the setup explains.

To go back to your TV's normal launcher at any time, just press Home twice, or turn the
option off in **Settings → Controls**.

## What is useful to report

Anything that looks wrong, feels slow, or does not do what you expected. Specifically
helpful:

- **Which TV or device you have**, and its Android version
  (Settings → System → About on most TVs)
- What you were doing when it happened
- A photo of the screen if it is something visual

Things worth trying: browsing your apps, moving apps around, the Continue Watching row,
the screensaver, themes and wallpapers, and the settings screens.

Two things I would especially like eyes on:

- **The Home button**, if you turned that option on — does pressing it always bring the
  launcher up, from any app, every time? A press that appears to do nothing is worth
  reporting straight away.
- **Scrolling through all your apps** — whether rows are ever cut off oddly at the top or
  bottom of the screen, and whether it ever stutters.

**Send reports to:** firefliesstudios.ca@gmail.com

## Getting updates

New versions install automatically once you are a tester. If you want one sooner, open
the Play Store on the TV, go to **My apps**, and check for updates.

## Leaving the test

Open the step 2 link again and choose **Leave the programme**, then leave the group from
step 1. The test build will be removed at the next update.
